## 2026-10-26 - Prevent Information Leakage in Production Logs\n**Vulnerability:** Use of `print()` statements exposes sensitive error details in production environments.\n**Learning:** In Flutter, `print()` outputs to the system log (e.g., Logcat) even in release builds, potentially leaking information.\n**Prevention:** Always use `debugPrint()` wrapped with `if (kDebugMode)` for general logging to ensure logs are only emitted during development.
