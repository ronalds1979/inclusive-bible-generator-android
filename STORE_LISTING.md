# Google Play Store Listing — Inclusive Bible Generator

## 1. English (`en-US`)

**App Name (max 30 chars):**
Inclusive Bible Generator

**Short Description (max 80 chars):**
Create accessible Bible verse images with alt-text, captions, and TTS audio.

**Full Description:**
Share God's Word in a way everyone can experience.

**Inclusive Bible Generator** helps you create beautiful, accessible Bible verse graphics for social media—complete with ready-to-copy Alternative Text (Alt-Text), inclusive captions, and Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio narration—100% free with no API keys required.

**Designed for Accessibility & Ease of Use:**
• **Ready to Share on Launch:** Automatically loads the Verse of the Day when you open the app (and reloads it when you switch languages)—pre-populating the preview, Alt-Text, and caption right away.
• **Guided 4-Step Flow:** Follow a simple step-by-step path—1. Verse, 2. Background, 3. Style, 4. Audio & Text—with live progress announcements, 48dp+ touch targets, `-`/`+` stepper buttons on every slider, and TalkBack/keyboard support.
• **Smart Bible Picker, Topic Search & Voice Input:** Pick any book, chapter, and verse range, search verses by keyword, phrase, or 1-tap themes (Peace, Hope, Love, Strength, Faith), or speak a Bible reference hands-free with voice input.
• **Rich Offline Typography & Style Presets:** Choose from 8 offline fonts (including Playfair Display, Lora, and Montserrat), Bold/Italic, Left/Center/Right alignment, luminance-aware shadows, Frosted & High-Contrast text backdrops, editorial card accents, line spacing, and 1-tap presets including **Dyslexia-Friendly**.
• **Built for Color-Blindness & Low Vision:** Always-visible named color swatches, live color naming, color-blindness-aware WCAG contrast checks (Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia), 1-tap **Suggest Safe Color**, **Best Position** smart placement, a **High-Contrast App Theme**, **Color-Blind-Friendly Status Colors**, and a **Full-Screen Vision Simulator Preview**.
• **Editable Alt-Text, Inclusivity Scorecard & Optional Free Gemini Tools:** Edit image descriptions inline, check your **4/4 Inclusivity Readiness** scorecard before sharing, and optionally enable zero-cost **On-Device Gemini Nano Alt-Text** (on compatible devices) or **Continue in Gemini App** handoff.
• **Audio Narration (TTS) with Word-Highlighted Captions:** Preview and export spoken audio in Male or Female device voices, with real-time word-highlighted transcripts for deaf and hard-of-hearing users.
• **Multilingual:** Full interface and Bible support in English (`EN-US`), Português (`PT-BR`), and Español (`ES-ES`).

**Acknowledgments:**
Special thanks to **YouVersion** for sharing the YouVersion Platform API that brings Bible books, translations, verses, and the daily Verse of the Day to this app.

## Google Play Data Safety Summary
- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** No.
- **Data stored locally on device only:** Per-language Bible version preferences (`preferred_bible_versions.json`), typography preferences (`typography_style.json`), and optional accessibility/advanced feature toggles (`advanced_features.json`) are stored locally in app support storage. None of this data is transmitted to the developer.
- **On-device processing:** Image rendering, pixel-sampled WCAG contrast analysis, vision simulation previews, Text-to-Speech narration, and optional Gemini Nano image description (ML Kit GenAI) run locally on the user's device.
- **Third-party requests:** Bible verse lookups, keyword/theme searches, and Verse of the Day requests are sent directly from the user's device to YouVersion Platform / Bolls.life / GetBible v2 only for the Scripture content requested by the user. Optional **Continue in Gemini App** handoff only shares a prompt when the user explicitly taps the button.
