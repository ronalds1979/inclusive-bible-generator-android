# Google Play Store Listing — Inclusive Bible Generator

## 1. English (`en-US`)

**App Name (max 30 chars):**
Inclusive Bible Generator

**Short Description (max 80 chars):**
Create accessible Bible verse images with alt-text, captions, and TTS audio.

**Full Description:**
Share God's Word in a way everyone can experience.

**Inclusive Bible Generator** helps you create beautiful, accessible Bible verse graphics for social media—complete with ready-to-copy Alternative Text (Alt-Text), inclusive captions, and Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio narration.

**Designed for Accessibility & Ease of Use:**
• **Ready to Share on Launch:** Automatically loads the Verse of the Day when you open the app—pre-populating the preview, Alt-Text, and caption right away.
• **Guided 4-Step Flow:** Follow a simple step-by-step path—1. Verse, 2. Background, 3. Style, 4. Audio & Text—with live progress announcements, 48dp+ touch targets, and keyboard/TalkBack support.
• **Smart Bible Picker:** Search any book by name and choose Chapter, Verse, or a Verse Range from dynamic dropdown lists, or refresh the Verse of the Day in one tap.
• **Accessible Visuals & Contrast:** Pick high-contrast text colors (with preset accessible swatches or a custom color picker), adjust font size (starting at a balanced 22pt default), dim busy backgrounds for legibility, position text via slider or drag, and choose 1:1, 3:4, 9:16, or 4:3 aspect ratios.
• **Stock, Gallery & Experimental AI Backgrounds:** Choose from built-in serene backgrounds, search free stock photos, pick a photo from your gallery, or enable the optional experimental Gemini AI background generator with your own API key.
• **Automatic Alt-Text & Captions:** Every generated image includes structured, screen-reader-friendly Alt-Text and social media captions ready to copy in one tap.
• **Audio Narration (TTS) with Live Transcript:** Preview and export spoken audio in Male or Female device voices, complete with a readable Audio Transcript and visual playback captions for deaf and hard-of-hearing users.
• **Multilingual:** Full interface and Bible support in English, Português (Brasil), and Español.

## Google Play Data Safety Summary
- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** No.
- **Data stored locally on device only:** Optional user-provided Gemini API key and YouVersion App Key are stored encrypted on-device using Android Keystore (`flutter_secure_storage`) and are never transmitted to the developer.
- **Third-party requests:** Bible verse lookups (including the Verse of the Day on launch), free stock photo searches, and optional AI background generation are sent directly from the user's device to GetBible / YouVersion / stock photo / Google Gemini APIs only for the content requested by the user.
