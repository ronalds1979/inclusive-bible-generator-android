Accessibility Statement

Inclusive Bible Generator (com.ronalds1979.inclusivebible)
Last reviewed: November 21, 2026

Conformance Target: Inclusive Bible Generator is designed and tested to conform with WCAG 2.1 / 2.2 Level AA, European Standard EN 301 549 v3.2.1 (Chapter 11 Non-Web Software & Chapter 9 Web), and the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882).

Built-in Accessibility Features (EN 301 549 Clause 12.1.1)

  • Automatic OS Preference Support: Automatically honors system High Contrast mode, Dark/Light theme, 200%+ system font scaling, and Reduced Motion settings.
  • Screen Reader & Language Support: Localized UI and Material semantics in English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese, semantic section headings, live-region status updates, and passage language attributes.
  • Keyboard, Switch Access & Motor Accessibility: Minimum 48×48dp touch targets, visible focus indicators, Arrow/Home keyboard repositioning on the verse preview, custom TalkBack actions, and direct Hex/RGB color entry without dragging.
  • Color-Vision & Low-Vision Tools: Real-pixel WCAG AA/AAA & color-blindness (Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia) contrast evaluation, built-in Vision Simulator, Color-Blind-Friendly status colors, Dyslexia-Friendly preset, and High-Contrast text backdrops.
  • Audio & Alternative Formats: Adjustable-rate Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio narration with live word-highlighted transcripts, persistent Stop Audio control, Voice-to-Verse dictation, and auto-generated Alt-Text & social captions.

Assessment Method & Verification

Conformance is evaluated through a combination of self-assessment, automated Flutter accessibility guideline checks (androidTapTargetGuideline, labeledTapTargetGuideline, textContrastGuideline, 200% text scaling, and compact landscape reflow tests), and manual testing with Android Accessibility Scanner, TalkBack, Switch Access, Voice Access, and physical keyboards.

Known Limitations

  • Voice-to-Verse dictation and on-device Gemini Nano image descriptions depend on Android system services available on the user's device. Manual text entry and manual Alt-Text editing are always available as full alternatives.

Feedback, Support & Enforcement Procedure (EAA, EN 301 549 Clause 12.2 & Spain RD 1112/2018)

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need documentation in an alternative format, please contact us:

  • Email: accessibility@inclusivebible.app (no account required)
  • Issue Tracker: GitHub Issues

We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback within 5 business days. If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may escalate your concern to the relevant national digital accessibility enforcement body in your EU Member State.

Declaración de Accesibilidad (Español)

Inclusive Bible Generator está diseñado para cumplir con WCAG 2.1 / 2.2 Nivel AA, la norma europea EN 301 549, la Ley Europea de Accesibilidad (Directiva (UE) 2019/882) y los criterios técnicos del Real Decreto 1112/2018. Respeta automáticamente el alto contraste del sistema, el movimiento reducido, la escala de texto al 200%, TalkBack/VoiceOver, teclado, Voice Access y Switch Access, simulador de daltonismo, dictado por voz, velocidad ajustable de audio con transcripción en vivo, espaciado de letras y generación automática de Alt-Text.

Método de evaluación: Autoevaluación combinada con pruebas automatizadas de accesibilidad (tamaño de objetivos táctiles de 48dp, contraste de color, etiquetas únicas y reflujo al 200%) y pruebas manuales con Android Accessibility Scanner, TalkBack, Switch Access y Voice Access.

Comunicaciones, quejas y procedimiento de reclamación: Para reportar barreras de accesibilidad, solicitar información en formato accesible o presentar una queja sin necesidad de crear una cuenta, escribe a accessibility@inclusivebible.app o abre una incidencia en GitHub. Responderemos en un plazo máximo de 5 días laborables. En caso de disconformidad con la respuesta, puedes dirigir una reclamación ante el organismo nacional competente en accesibilidad digital.

Declaração de Acessibilidade (Português — Brasil)

O Inclusive Bible Generator foi projetado para atender às diretrizes WCAG 2.1 / 2.2 Nível AA, à norma europeia EN 301 549 e à Lei Europeia de Acessibilidade (Diretiva (UE) 2019/882). O aplicativo respeita automaticamente alto contraste do sistema, movimento reduzido, fonte em 200%, TalkBack/VoiceOver, navegação por teclado, Voice Access e Switch Access, simulador de daltonismo, ditado por voz, velocidade ajustável de narração com transcrição ao vivo, espaçamento entre letras e geração automática de Alt-Text.

Método de avaliação: Autoavaliação combinada com testes automatizados de diretrizes de acessibilidade (alvos de toque de 48dp, contraste de cor, rótulos únicos e reflow em 200%) e verificação manual com Android Accessibility Scanner, TalkBack, Switch Access e Voice Access.

Feedback, suporte e reclamações: Para relatar barreiras de acessibilidade ou solicitar suporte sem precisar de conta, envie um e-mail para accessibility@inclusivebible.app ou abra uma issue no GitHub. Nosso objetivo é responder em até 5 dias úteis.
