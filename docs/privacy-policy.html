Privacy Policy

Inclusive Bible Generator (com.ronalds1979.inclusivebible)
Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Summary: Inclusive Bible Generator does not collect, sell, or share personal data. All Bible graphics, alt-text, captions, and TTS audio are generated on your device, and any optional API keys you enter are stored encrypted locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The developer of Inclusive Bible Generator does not operate backend servers and does not collect, track, or store your personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or generated content.

2. Locally Stored Data (API Keys)

If you choose to connect an optional Google Gemini API Key or YouVersion Platform App Key, that key is stored exclusively on your device using Android's encrypted storage (flutter_secure_storage). You can remove your key at any time inside the app.

3. Photos, Media & Audio

  • Gallery Access: If you pick a custom background image from your device gallery, the image is read locally to render your verse graphic and is never uploaded to the developer.
  • Export & Sharing: Generated images and Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio files are saved to your device or shared only when you explicitly choose to share them.

4. Third-Party Services

When you tap actions that require an internet connection, your device communicates directly with the respective service:

  • Bible Text Services (GetBible / YouVersion / Bible-API): Used to fetch the Bible verse or Verse of the Day you request.
  • Google Gemini API (Optional): Only used if you connect your own Gemini API key and tap Generate Background with Gemini. Requests are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

Inclusive Bible Generator does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the project repository: github.com/ronalds1979/inclusive-bible-generator-android.
