Privacy Policy

Inclusive Bible Generator (com.ronalds1979.inclusivebible)
Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Summary: Inclusive Bible Generator does not collect, sell, or share personal data. All Bible graphics, alt-text, captions, transcripts, contrast checks, Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio, and optional Gemini Nano image descriptions run locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The developer of Inclusive Bible Generator does not operate backend servers and does not collect, track, or store your personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or generated content.

2. Locally Stored Preferences

Your preferred Bible version per language (preferred_bible_versions.json), typography style settings (typography_style.json), and optional accessibility or advanced feature toggles (advanced_features.json) are stored exclusively in local app storage on your device and are never transmitted to the developer.

3. Photos, Voice Input & Audio

  • Gallery Access: If you pick a custom background image from your device gallery, the image is read locally to render your verse graphic, sample contrast, and optionally generate an on-device Alt-Text description. It is never uploaded to the developer.
  • On-Device Gemini Nano (Optional): On compatible Android devices, enabling On-Device Alt-Text (Gemini Nano) uses Android's local ML Kit GenAI / AICore service to describe your background image entirely on-device.
  • Voice-to-Verse Input (Optional): When you tap the microphone button to speak a Bible reference, Android's system speech recognition service processes your voice request and returns only the recognized reference text to the app.
  • Export & Sharing: Generated images, Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio files, and optional prompts shared via Continue in Gemini App are saved or shared only when you explicitly tap a share or open action.

4. Third-Party Services

When the app fetches the Verse of the Day on startup or when you tap actions that require an internet connection, your device communicates directly with the respective service:

  • Bible Text & Search Services (YouVersion Platform / Bolls.life / GetBible v2): Used to fetch the Verse of the Day on launch or when switching languages, retrieve Bible books, translations, and verses, and search verses by keyword or theme.
  • Continue in Gemini App (Optional): Only used if you enable the toggle and explicitly tap Share to Gemini or Copy & Open Gemini, handing a prompt directly to your own Gemini app or gemini.google.com, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

Inclusive Bible Generator does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the Inclusive Bible Generator GitHub repository.
